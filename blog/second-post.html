BP02: Open Source Reflection

[November 5, 2026]

Now that midterms are over, I am able to reflect on how I have grown as a software engineer this semester and how I can grow. Having to balance coursework, labor, and everday responsibilites has been a challenge, but with this I can see what worked and what needs improvement.

Reflection: Growth, Challenges, and Solutions

Growth:

Heading into the second half of the semester, I feel like I have had a good amount of hands-on experience and some good technical approach planning as a software engineer. I have been working with Pyhton, Javascript, and html and getting some good version control practice while working on BCSR and CAS. This has helped strengthen my code structure - making sure not to mix files, but instead keeping a good structure and seperating things like html and inline javascript. Another thing I have learned is how to get more efficient in digging into new repos to contribute. For example, when I began to work in CAS, I had to debug all the outdated dependencies and get the setup working as intended. This time it did not take as long as it did for BCSR.

Challenges:

One of my biggest challenges this semester has been balancing my time between school, work, and personal life. I have had to learn how to manage my time better and prioritize my tasks. Time management is my main setback this semester. And waiting and setting back contributions until I have a larger block of available time rather than just trying to break into smaller sprints has slowed me down. Sickness has still also been a challenge for me this semester. I have had to put school aside in order to focus on the well-being of myself and my family.
